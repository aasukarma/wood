Quick Answers

FAQs — Wooden Door Manufacturing

What types of wooden doors does Motion Woods manufacture in Pune?

We manufacture teakwood and veneer main doors, panel/veneer/laminate bedroom doors, waterproof flush and WPC/PVC toilet doors, acoustic conference room doors, antibacterial laminate hospital doors, classroom doors and heavy-duty hostel doors — with frames, installation and finishing included.

Can you manufacture doors on-site for a multi-tower project?

Yes. On-site manufacturing is our core model for builder projects: we install machinery at your site and deploy a dedicated 20–25 worker team, which reduces transport damage and keeps finishing consistent across thousands of doors.

What is the typical delivery timeline for bulk door orders?

Timelines depend on quantity and specification, but as a reference the legacy team completed 3150+ doors and 1800+ frames across 8 towers in about 1.5 years at Roshan Milestone. Share your BOQ on WhatsApp for a specific schedule.

Do you supply doors to individual homeowners or only builders?

Both. We serve builders and developers at township scale, and also manufacture custom teakwood and veneer doors for bungalows, villas and individual apartments across Pune.
