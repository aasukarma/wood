The Story Behind Motion Woods

A new-generation company powered by 35+ years of craftsmanship — from a village workshop in Uttar Pradesh to Pune's largest residential townships.

Story of Root

From Heritage to High-Rise

Our founder, Diwakar Rajpat Vishwakarma, is a distinguished name symbolizing the fusion of traditional craftsmanship and contemporary design. Emerging from a small village in Uttar Pradesh, he has preserved a legacy nurtured for generations through the art of woodworking.

From a young age, he grew up watching his father create art from wood. A deep connection to craftsmanship in his formative years, followed by the opportunity to work alongside renowned architects in Mumbai, expanded his perspective to a global level.

Through his proprietorship firm Diwakar Furniture, he has spent 35+ years manufacturing and installing doors, furniture and complete interiors for Pune's most demanding developers — including 7,650+ doors across just two townships, plus projects for Pride Purple Group, Yashada Realty, Roshan Realty, VTP Realty, Gera Developments, Bhate & Raje and many more.

Motion Woods Private Limited is the next chapter: the same master craftsman, the same trained site teams and industrial machinery, now structured as a private limited company built for larger contracts, tighter processes and long-term partnerships with builders and institutions.

What is the relationship between Motion Woods and Diwakar Furniture?

Diwakar Furniture is the proprietorship firm founded by Diwakar R. Vishwakarma, backed by his 35+ years of door and furniture manufacturing across Pune and Mumbai. Motion Woods Private Limited is the new corporate entity that carries forward the same founder, team, machinery and quality standards under a professionally structured company.

Who leads Motion Woods Private Limited?

Diwakar R. Vishwakarma (Director & Founder) and Pramodkumar R. Gupta (Director) lead the board, with Anshu Diwakar Vishwakarma as Chief Executive Officer driving operations and growth.
