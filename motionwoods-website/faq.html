Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about Motion Woods, our door manufacturing model, builder contracts, interiors and finishing.

About the Company

Who is Motion Woods Private Limited?

Motion Woods Private Limited is a Pune-based wooden door and furniture manufacturing company serving residential, commercial and institutional projects. It is built on the legacy of Diwakar Furniture, the proprietorship firm of founder Diwakar R. Vishwakarma, who brings 35+ years of woodworking experience.

Is Motion Woods a new company? How can it claim 35+ years of experience?

Motion Woods Private Limited is a newly structured corporate entity, but its founder, core team, machinery and processes come from Diwakar Furniture — a proprietorship backed by the founder's 35+ years in woodworking, whose team delivered 7,650+ doors across just two townships (Roshan Milestone and Yashada Splendid Park) for leading Pune developers. The experience belongs to the people and systems now operating as Motion Woods.

Who are the directors and leadership of Motion Woods?

Diwakar R. Vishwakarma is Director & Founder, Pramodkumar R. Gupta is Director, and Anshu Diwakar Vishwakarma serves as Chief Executive Officer.

Where is Motion Woods located and which areas do you serve?

Our registered office is in Hadapsar, Pune – 411028, and our 12,000 sq ft manufacturing factory is at Nere, near Punawale, Pune. We serve projects across Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad and the wider Maharashtra region.

Doors & Manufacturing

What types of doors do you manufacture?

Teakwood and veneer main doors, panel/veneer/laminate bedroom doors, waterproof flush and WPC/PVC toilet doors, office cabin doors, acoustic conference room doors, antibacterial laminate hospital doors, classroom doors and heavy-duty hostel flush doors.

What is on-site door manufacturing and why does it matter?

Instead of shipping finished doors, we install machinery (hydraulic press, laminating, multi-boring) at the project site and manufacture frames and shutters there. This eliminates transport damage, syncs production with the construction schedule, and keeps finishing consistent across thousands of doors.

What machinery does Motion Woods operate?

NMT-50T hydraulic press, sliding table saw, post forming machine and MAX WOOD multi-head boring machine — industrial-grade equipment for panel lamination, precision cutting, edge profiles and hardware fitting at scale.

Does Motion Woods have its own factory?

Yes — a 12,000 sq ft manufacturing facility at Nere, near Punawale, Pune, housing the hydraulic press, sliding table saw, post forming and multi-boring lines. For township contracts, we additionally set up machinery directly at the builder's site.

Do you provide door installation or only manufacturing?

Both. Frames, shutters, hardware fitting, installation and final polishing are handled by one accountable team under one contract.

For Builders & Developers

What scale of builder projects can you handle?

Multi-tower projects are our core model. Reference deliveries: 3150+ doors and 1800+ frames across 8 towers at Roshan Milestone in 1.5 years; 4500+ doors and 2650+ frames across 9 towers at Yashada Splendid Park in 2 years; 65%+ of door work across the Pride Purple Park Street township.

How large is your site team for a township project?

A dedicated team of 20–25 trained workers per major site, with supervisors, quality control and on-site machinery operation included.

Homeowners, Interiors & Finishing

Do you take individual home and bungalow projects?

Yes. From single custom teakwood doors to complete bungalow interiors — the legacy team delivered a ₹2 Cr, 8-bedroom bungalow at Undri in 6 months, including kitchens, mandirs, staircases and full finishing.

What finishing options do you offer?

PU, Melamine, Duco and matte polishing, veneer polishing, Deco paints, designer wall textures and protective coatings — applied with a zero-drip, dust-controlled process.

Do you make modular kitchens?

Yes — modular and traditional kitchens with premium hardware (Hettich), quartz countertops, tall units and accessories.

How do I prevent my wooden doors from warping in Pune's monsoon?

Choose properly seasoned wood or engineered cores, seal all six faces (including top and bottom edges), and ensure PU or Melamine coating. Read our detailed guide on preventing monsoon door warping on the blog.
