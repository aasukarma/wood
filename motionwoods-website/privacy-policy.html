Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with Motion Woods Private Limited.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Motion Woods Private Limited ("Motion Woods", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website motionwoods.com and our enquiry forms, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using this website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Motion Woods Private Limited is a wooden door, furniture and interiors manufacturer with its registered office at Hadapsar, Pune – 411028, Maharashtra, India. For any privacy-related request you can reach us at admin@motionwoods.com or +91 98607 85024.

What information we collect

  • Details you give us through our contact form, interiors enquiry form or cost estimator — typically your name, mobile number, city/locality, and details about your project or requirement.
  • Messages you send us directly via WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Basic technical information your browser shares automatically, such as device type and pages visited, used only to keep the site working and understand general usage.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as financial account numbers, government IDs or passwords through this website, and you should not share them with us here.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, share a quotation or estimate, and arrange consultations or site visits.
  • To provide and manage the products and services you ask for.
  • To contact you about your project by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To improve our website and services.

We use your information only for the purposes for which you provided it, or for closely related purposes.

WhatsApp and third-party services

Our enquiry forms are designed to open a pre-filled message in WhatsApp so you can send your details to our team. When you do this, your message is delivered through WhatsApp / Meta and is also subject to their own privacy terms. We also use Google Fonts to display this website. These third parties may process limited technical data as part of providing their service.

How we store and retain your information

We keep your information only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and serve you, and to meet any legal or accounting requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised. We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect it, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with our own team and with trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, communication or hosting providers), and where required by law.

Your rights

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to access, correct, update or delete the personal information you have shared, and you may withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time. To exercise any of these rights, or to raise a grievance, email admin@motionwoods.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This website uses only minimal cookies or similar technologies needed for basic functionality and general analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

This website is intended for adults making home or business decisions and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Please review it periodically.
