Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Built for Tomorrow

Motion Woods was born from a deep-rooted legacy of woodworking that spans generations. Our founder, Diwakar Vishwakarma, grew up in a small village in Uttar Pradesh watching his father transform raw wood into art — and brought that vision to the cities of Maharashtra.

Over 35+ years in the craft, through his proprietorship firm Diwakar Furniture, he manufactured and installed thousands of doors and complete interiors for Pune's most prestigious developers. Motion Woods Private Limited carries that same team, machinery and standard of excellence into a new, professionally structured company — now operating from a 12,000 sq ft factory at Jambe, near Punawale.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Motion Woods Private Limited?

Motion Woods Private Limited is a Pune-based wooden door and furniture manufacturing company. It is built on the legacy of Diwakar Furniture — the proprietorship firm of founder Diwakar R. Vishwakarma, who brings 35+ years of woodworking experience — whose team has delivered 7,650+ doors across just two township projects — Roshan Milestone and Yashada Splendid Park — among many more for leading Pune developers including Pride Purple, Yashada Realty, Roshan Realty, VTP Realty and Gera Developments. The company operates a 12,000 sq ft manufacturing factory at Jambe, near Punawale, Pune.

What services does Motion Woods offer in Pune?

Motion Woods offers wooden door manufacturing and installation, furniture manufacturing, interior furnishing, on-site carpentry, wood polishing and finishing (PU, Melamine, Duco), and large-scale builder and township door contracts across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Does Motion Woods handle bulk door contracts for builders?

Yes. On-site manufacturing setup with dedicated teams of 20–25 workers is our core model for multi-tower residential projects — the legacy team has delivered projects like Roshan Milestone (3150+ doors, 8 towers) and Yashada Splendid Park (4500+ doors, 9 towers).
