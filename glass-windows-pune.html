Windows, Glass & Glazing

uPVC and aluminium windows, toughened glass, partitions and façade glazing for Pune homes and offices — measured, supplied and fitted by one accountable team.

Motion Woods Glass & Windows

Light, air and quiet — done right

Good windows keep the dust, rain and traffic noise out while letting light and air in. We help you pick the right system for your flat, bungalow or office, then measure, supply and install it cleanly.

From a single window replacement to full-home glazing and office partitions, everything is handled under one contract — no chasing separate fabricators and fitters.
