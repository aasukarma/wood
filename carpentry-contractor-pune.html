Carpentry & Custom Work in Pune

Skilled on-site carpentry with factory-finish quality — kitchens, paneling, staircases, mandirs and bespoke woodwork built to your space.

What We Execute

  • On-site carpentry with skilled workmanship
  • Custom furniture manufacturing as per design & space
  • Wardrobes, beds, TV units, shoe racks & storage units
  • Modular & traditional kitchen carpentry
  • Wall paneling, partitions & decorative wooden elements
  • Wooden staircases – installation of treads & risers, matching colour tint and grain enhancement
  • Custom mandirs – Corian CNC design & carved teakwood

How We Work

  • Factory-finish quality with precise installation
  • High-grade hardware & long-lasting materials

Quick Answers

FAQs — Carpentry & Custom Work

Do you provide carpenters for on-site work in Pune?

Yes. On-site carpentry execution is our core strength — trained teams work at your site with dust-controlled processes, high-grade hardware and dedicated supervision.

Can you build a custom teakwood mandir or staircase?

Yes. We craft custom mandirs in carved teakwood and Corian CNC designs, and install wooden staircases with grain-matched treads and risers.

What is the difference between your carpentry and a local carpenter?

Scale, machinery and accountability: industrial pressing and cutting machines, trained multi-person teams, written scope and timelines, and finishing standards proven across 7,650+ doors in two townships and dozens of interiors.
