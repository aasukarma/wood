Factory finish, site-fit precision

Furniture built by Motion Woods uses high-grade hardware (Hettich and equivalent), machine-pressed panels and PU/Melamine finishing. Because our carpenters execute on-site, every unit is fitted to the actual wall, floor and ceiling — not to a drawing that ignores site tolerances.

Quick Answers

FAQs — Furniture Manufacturing

What kinds of furniture does Motion Woods manufacture?

We manufacture residential furniture (beds, wardrobes, modular kitchens, TV units, dining tables), commercial furniture (workstations, reception counters, conference tables) and institutional furniture (classroom benches, lab tables, library racks, hostel cots) in Pune.

Do you make custom furniture to design?

Yes. We build custom furniture as per your design and space, using premium hardware like Hettich, quartz countertops for kitchens, and PU or Melamine finishes.

Can you furnish an entire school, hospital or office?

Yes. Institutional fit-outs are a core capability — the legacy team has furnished schools like City Pride School Ravet and corporate offices including Kritija Construction's head office.
