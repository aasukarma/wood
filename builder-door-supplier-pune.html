Door Partner for Builders & Townships

We don't ship doors to your site — we build the door factory at your site. On-site machinery, dedicated teams and QC across every tower.

The on-site manufacturing model

For multi-tower residential projects, transporting finished doors invites damage, rework and schedule risk. Our model: install the machinery — hydraulic press, laminating, boring — inside your project, deploy a dedicated 20–25 worker team, and manufacture frames and shutters floor by floor as towers become ready.

  • Faster: production syncs with your slab cycle; no waiting on factory dispatch.
  • Consistent: one team, one setup, identical finish across thousands of units.
  • Accountable: manufacturing, installation, hardware and polishing under one contract.

For small and mid-size orders, our 12,000 sq ft factory at Jambe, near Punawale handles production with careful delivery logistics — so the model scales down as well as up.

Quick Answers

FAQs — Builder & Township Projects

How does on-site door manufacturing work for builders?

Motion Woods installs woodworking machinery (hydraulic press, laminating, multi-boring) inside your project site and deploys a dedicated 20–25 worker team. Frames and shutters are manufactured and installed floor by floor, synced with your construction schedule — eliminating transport damage and dispatch delays.

What scale of projects can Motion Woods handle?

The team's track record covers 8–9 tower projects: 3150+ doors at Roshan Milestone (1.5 years) and 4500+ doors at Yashada Splendid Park (2 years), plus 65%+ of the Park Street township for Pride Purple Group.

How do we get a quotation for a builder project?

Share your BOQ, door schedule and project timeline on WhatsApp (+91 98607 85024) or through the contact form. We'll visit the site, confirm specifications and submit a detailed commercial proposal.
